# Candidate Profiles

*Candidate information brief · Andhra Pradesh People's Alliance · March 2024*

## Smt. Lakshmi Devi Nallapaneni — Vijayawada Central, NTR District

Smt. Lakshmi Devi Nallapaneni is the Alliance candidate for the Vijayawada Central
assembly constituency in NTR district.

**Born.** 14 August 1974, in Gudivada, Krishna district.

**Education.** B.Com from Andhra Loyola College, Vijayawada, 1994. LL.B from
Andhra University, 1998. Enrolled with the Andhra Pradesh Bar Council in 1999.

**Profession.** Practising advocate at the Vijayawada district court for
twenty-three years, specialising in land title and tenancy disputes. Founding
trustee of the Krishna Delta Tenant Farmers' Legal Aid Trust, which has provided
free representation in 1,340 tenancy cases since 2009.

**Political career.** Ward member, Vijayawada Municipal Corporation, 2005 to 2010.
Chairperson of the Corporation's Standing Committee on Water Supply, 2007 to 2010.
State convenor of the Alliance's Women's Wing since 2019. First contested the
assembly in 2019, losing Vijayawada Central by 4,116 votes.

**Priorities for the constituency.** Completion of the Budameru diversion channel to
end recurring flooding in Singh Nagar and Rajarajeswaripeta; a second drinking
water treatment plant to close the 24 million litres per day supply gap; and
decongestion of Benz Circle through the eastern bypass.

**Assets declaration.** Movable assets Rs. 41.2 lakh, immovable assets Rs. 2.14
crore, liabilities Rs. 18.6 lakh, as declared in the affidavit filed with the
Returning Officer. No criminal cases pending or past.

**Languages.** Telugu, English, Hindi.

## Sri Ramachandra Rao Vemuri — Mylavaram, NTR District

Sri Ramachandra Rao Vemuri is the Alliance candidate for the Mylavaram assembly
constituency in NTR district.

**Born.** 2 February 1969, in Kanchikacherla, NTR district.

**Education.** B.Sc Agriculture from Acharya N.G. Ranga Agricultural University,
Bapatla, 1990. Post-graduate diploma in agri-business management, 1993.

**Profession.** Farmer and agricultural input business owner. Chairman of the
Mylavaram Primary Agricultural Cooperative Credit Society, 2011 to 2019.

**Political career.** Sarpanch of Kanchikacherla gram panchayat, 1995 to 2001.
Zilla Parishad Territorial Constituency member for Mylavaram, 2006 to 2011. Member
of the Legislative Assembly for Mylavaram, 2014 to 2019. Served on the Assembly
Committee on Agriculture and Irrigation.

**Priorities for the constituency.** Extension of the Chintalapudi lift irrigation
scheme to nine upland mandals classified semi-critical for groundwater; cold storage
capacity for the mango and cotton belt; and upgrading the Mylavaram area hospital
from 30 to 100 beds.

**Assets declaration.** Movable assets Rs. 63.8 lakh, immovable assets Rs. 4.71
crore including 11.5 acres of agricultural land, liabilities Rs. 92.4 lakh. No
criminal cases pending or past.

**Languages.** Telugu, English.

## Dr. Ayesha Begum Shaik — Guntur West, Guntur District

Dr. Ayesha Begum Shaik is the Alliance candidate for the Guntur West assembly
constituency in Guntur district.

**Born.** 27 November 1981, in Guntur.

**Education.** MBBS from Guntur Medical College, 2005. MD in Community Medicine from
Osmania Medical College, Hyderabad, 2009.

**Profession.** Public health physician. District Immunisation Officer, Guntur,
2012 to 2017. Led the Guntur district COVID-19 containment cell in 2020 and 2021.
Author of the state's 2018 anaemia reduction protocol for adolescent girls.

**Political career.** First-time candidate. Joined the Alliance in 2022 as convenor
of its Health Policy Advisory Group.

**Priorities for the constituency.** Twenty-four-hour staffing of all eleven urban
Primary Health Centres in Guntur West; a dedicated maternal and child health wing at
the Government General Hospital; and elimination of the 118 percent bed occupancy
overload through a satellite 200-bed facility.

**Assets declaration.** Movable assets Rs. 28.4 lakh, immovable assets Rs. 1.32
crore, liabilities Rs. 31.0 lakh. No criminal cases pending or past.

**Languages.** Telugu, Urdu, English, Hindi.

## Sri Bhaskar Naidu Kondepudi — Visakhapatnam North, Visakhapatnam District

Sri Bhaskar Naidu Kondepudi is the Alliance candidate for the Visakhapatnam North
assembly constituency.

**Born.** 9 June 1966, in Anakapalli.

**Education.** B.E in Mechanical Engineering from Andhra University College of
Engineering, 1988.

**Profession.** Retired Deputy General Manager, Visakhapatnam Steel Plant, where he
served twenty-eight years and led the labour welfare committee for six.

**Political career.** General Secretary of the Steel Plant Employees' Union, 2003 to
2014. Member of the Visakhapatnam Municipal Corporation from 2015 to 2020.

**Priorities for the constituency.** Anchoring the 1.4 lakh IT and IT-enabled
services jobs commitment in the Visakhapatnam technology park; completion of the
outer ring road; and regularisation of tenure for 14,000 families in unauthorised
hill-slope colonies.

**Assets declaration.** Movable assets Rs. 87.6 lakh including retirement benefits,
immovable assets Rs. 2.05 crore, liabilities nil. No criminal cases pending or past.

**Languages.** Telugu, English, Hindi, Odia.

## How Candidates Are Selected

Candidates are recommended by the district selection committee after consultation
with mandal-level office bearers, and confirmed by the state parliamentary board.
The Alliance requires every candidate to publish a full assets and criminal-record
affidavit, and does not field candidates with pending charges for offences against
women, corruption, or communal incitement.

Women constitute 33 percent of the Alliance's candidate list across the 175
assembly constituencies.
