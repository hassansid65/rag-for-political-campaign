# Welfare Schemes — Eligibility and Benefits Reference

*Scheme reference booklet · Andhra Pradesh People's Alliance · Version 2.1*

## Rythu Bharosa Plus

**Benefit.** Rs. 18,000 per farmer family per year, paid in three instalments of
Rs. 7,000 (May, before Kharif sowing), Rs. 7,000 (October, before Rabi sowing) and
Rs. 4,000 (January, at harvest).

**Eligibility.** Any family owning cultivable agricultural land in Andhra Pradesh,
regardless of holding size. Tenant farmers holding a valid Crop Cultivator Rights
Card (CCRC) issued for the current season. Families where any member is an
income-tax payer, a serving or retired government employee drawing a pension above
Rs. 10,000 per month, or an elected representative are excluded.

**How to apply.** Through the Village Agriculture Assistant at the Rythu Bharosa
Kendram in your gram panchayat. Documents required: Aadhaar, land passbook or CCRC,
and a bank account in the applicant's name. No application fee.

**Payment mode.** Direct Benefit Transfer to the Aadhaar-linked bank account.

## Amma Vodi

**Benefit.** Rs. 15,000 per year paid to the mother or recognised guardian of each
child enrolled in Class 1 to Class 12, capped at three children per household.

**Eligibility.** Household income below Rs. 2.5 lakh per annum. The child must be
enrolled in a government, aided, or recognised private school and maintain at least
75 percent attendance in the preceding academic year. The family must hold a rice
ration card. Land holding must not exceed 3 acres wet or 10 acres dry.

**How to apply.** No separate application. Enrolment is drawn automatically from
the school child-info database. Verify your entry with the head teacher during the
social audit displayed at the school in November.

**Payment mode.** Single annual credit in June to the mother's bank account.

## Dr. YSR Aarogyasri

**Benefit.** Cashless treatment up to Rs. 10 lakh per family per year across 4,100
listed procedures at empanelled hospitals. Includes paediatric cardiac surgery,
cochlear implants, cancer immunotherapy, dialysis and organ transplants.

**Eligibility.** Any family holding a white ration card, or with annual household
income below Rs. 5 lakh. There is no age limit and no waiting period. Pre-existing
conditions are covered from day one.

**How to apply.** Present your Aarogyasri card or ration card with Aadhaar at the
Aarogyamitra help desk of any empanelled hospital. For planned procedures, obtain a
referral from the Primary Health Centre or Area Hospital.

**Post-operative support.** Aarogya Aasara pays Rs. 5,000 to Rs. 10,000 per month
during the prescribed convalescence period for 51 listed procedures.

| Fisherman | Rs. 4,000 |

| Disability 40 to 79 percent | Rs. 4,000 |

| Chronic renal failure on dialysis | Rs. 10,000 |

**Eligibility.** Household income below Rs. 10,000 per month in rural areas or
Rs. 12,000 per month in urban areas. Land holding not exceeding 3 acres wet or
10 acres dry. No four-wheeler in the household except a taxi or tractor.

**Delivery.** Paid at the doorstep by the Village or Ward Volunteer on the first of
every month, before 11 a.m. If not received by the 3rd, call 1902.

## Jagananna Vidya Deevena — Fee Reimbursement

**Benefit.** Full tuition fee reimbursement for ITI, polytechnic, degree, and
professional courses, paid directly to the institution.

**Eligibility.** Household income below Rs. 2.5 lakh per annum, land holding under
10 acres, and 75 percent attendance. Applies to students in recognised institutions
within Andhra Pradesh. One additional child per family is now covered.

**Timeline.** Released quarterly, within 60 days of the end of each quarter.

## Jagananna Vasathi Deevena — Maintenance Allowance

**Benefit.** Rs. 20,000 per year for degree and engineering students, Rs. 15,000 for
polytechnic, and Rs. 10,000 for ITI, toward hostel and mess charges. Paid in two
instalments to the mother's account.

## Housing — Navaratnalu Pedalandariki Illu

**Benefit.** Unit assistance of Rs. 2.6 lakh in plain areas and Rs. 3.1 lakh in
tribal and hilly mandals. Includes a free house site of 1.5 cents where the
beneficiary is landless.

**Eligibility.** No pucca house owned anywhere in India by any family member.
Household income below Rs. 10,000 per month rural or Rs. 12,000 urban. Registration
is compulsorily in the name of the woman of the household.

**Construction stages and payment.** Released in five tranches: basement, plinth,
roof level, roof cast, and completion, verified by the Housing Engineering
Assistant.

## Women's Self Help Group Support

**Benefit.** Loans up to Rs. 5 lakh per group at zero effective interest, with the
full interest subvention paid to the bank by the state. Additional Rs. 1 lakh
revolving fund for groups with a 24-month clean repayment record.

**Eligibility.** Registered DWCRA or Mahila Samakhya group of 10 to 20 members with
at least 12 months of regular savings and 90 percent internal repayment.

## Free Electricity

**Benefit.** Two hundred free units per month for domestic connections consuming
under 300 units per month. Nine hours of assured free daytime supply for
agricultural connections, on a published feeder schedule.

**Eligibility.** Domestic connection in the applicant's name, with a rice ration
card for the free-unit benefit. Agricultural benefit applies to all metered
agricultural service connections.

## Fishermen Support

**Benefit.** Diesel subsidy of Rs. 12 per litre up to 3,000 litres per mechanised
boat per year. Rs. 20,000 per family during the annual 61-day fishing ban.
Ex-gratia of Rs. 10 lakh for death at sea.

**Eligibility.** Membership of a registered fishermen cooperative society and a
valid boat registration. The ban-period payment requires the family to hold a
fishermen identity card issued by the Fisheries Department.
