# NTR District Profile — Vijayawada

*District information brief · Andhra Pradesh People's Alliance · Updated March 2024*

## Overview

NTR district was formed on 4 April 2022 from the erstwhile Krishna district. Its
headquarters is Vijayawada, historically known as Bezawada, which sits on the
north bank of the Krishna river and is the commercial capital of Andhra Pradesh.

## Assembly Constituencies

The seven assembly segments in NTR district are Vijayawada West, Vijayawada
Central, Vijayawada East, Mylavaram, Nandigama (SC), Jaggayyapeta and Tiruvuru
(SC). Nandigama and Tiruvuru are reserved for Scheduled Caste candidates.

Voter turnout in the 2019 general election was 78.6 percent across the district,
with Vijayawada Central recording the lowest at 71.2 percent and Tiruvuru the
highest at 84.9 percent.

## Economy

Vijayawada is a major trading hub for agricultural produce, particularly rice,
chilli and turmeric. The Vijayawada market yard handles roughly 4.2 lakh tonnes of
paddy per season. Other significant sectors are automobile dealerships,
construction materials, printing, and health services — the city has 68 registered
private hospitals serving a catchment extending into Krishna, Eluru, Guntur and
Bapatla districts.

Mylavaram and Jaggayyapeta mandals host cement manufacturing, with four plants and
a combined capacity of 9.6 million tonnes per year. Tiruvuru and Nandigama are
predominantly agricultural, with paddy, cotton and pulses under the Nagarjuna Sagar
left canal command.

## Irrigation and Water

The Prakasam Barrage, built across the Krishna at Vijayawada, feeds the Krishna
Western, Eastern and Ryves canals, irrigating approximately 13.08 lakh acres across
NTR, Krishna, Guntur and Bapatla districts. Nine mandals in the upland belt —
including Mylavaram, G. Konduru and Chandarlapadu — depend on borewells and are
classified as semi-critical for groundwater.

Vijayawada city receives its drinking water from the Krishna river via the
Bhavanipuram and Ramalingeswara Nagar treatment plants, with a combined capacity of
114 million litres per day against an assessed demand of 138 MLD.

## Infrastructure

Vijayawada Junction is one of the busiest railway junctions in India, handling over
400 trains daily. Vijayawada International Airport at Gannavaram operates flights
to eleven domestic destinations and to Singapore. National Highways 16 and 65
intersect at Vijayawada, and the eastern bypass is under construction.

The proposed Vijayawada Metro Rail, Phase 1, covers 26.03 kilometres across two
corridors: Pandit Nehru Bus Station to Gannavaram, and Pandit Nehru Bus Station to
Penamaluru. The detailed project report values Phase 1 at Rs. 11,009 crore. Central
government sanction is pending.

## Key Local Issues

1. **Urban flooding.** The Budameru rivulet overflows into western Vijayawada
   during heavy monsoon, affecting Singh Nagar, Rajarajeswaripeta and Vambay
   Colony. The Budameru diversion channel widening is estimated at Rs. 480 crore.
2. **Drinking water in upland mandals.** Nine mandals require the Chintalapudi
   lift irrigation scheme extension for assured drinking water.
3. **Traffic congestion.** The Kanaka Durga flyover eased highway traffic but
   internal city congestion at Benz Circle and Ramavarappadu Ring remains acute.
4. **Sand and gravel availability.** Krishna riverbed sand reaches for
   construction supply require regulated de-silting permissions.
5. **Health infrastructure load.** The Government General Hospital Vijayawada
   operates at 118 percent of sanctioned bed occupancy.

## Commitments for NTR District

- The 300-bed super-speciality hospital at Vijayawada, sanctioned at Rs. 680 crore,
  will begin construction within the first year.
- Budameru diversion channel widening funded in full at Rs. 480 crore, with work
  starting before the 2026 monsoon.
- Vijayawada eastern bypass completed, with all state land acquisition concluded
  within eighteen months.
- 84 government schools in the district upgraded to English-medium model schools.
- Metro Rail Phase 1 pursued with the Union government, with the state's 20 percent
  equity share of Rs. 2,202 crore committed in the state budget.
- Free RTC bus travel for women extended across all 20 mandals.
