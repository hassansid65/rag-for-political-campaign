# Vision 2029 — Election Manifesto

*Published by the Andhra Pradesh People's Alliance · January 2024*

## Preamble

This manifesto sets out the commitments of the Andhra Pradesh People's Alliance to
the people of all twenty-six districts. Every promise in this document carries a
timeline and a funding source. We publish it so that citizens can hold us to it.

## Chapter 1 — Agriculture and Farmer Welfare

### Rythu Bharosa Plus

We will expand the Rythu Bharosa direct benefit transfer from Rs. 13,500 to
Rs. 18,000 per farmer family per year, paid in three instalments timed to the
Kharif sowing, the Rabi sowing, and the harvest. Tenant farmers holding a
Crop Cultivator Rights Card will be included for the first time, adding an
estimated 8.4 lakh families statewide.

### Irrigation

The Polavaram Irrigation Project will be completed to its full 45.72 metre height
by December 2027. In the Krishna delta, the Prakasam Barrage modernisation will be
funded at Rs. 1,240 crore, protecting the irrigated command area serving NTR,
Krishna, Bapatla and Guntur districts.

We will construct 1,200 new check dams across Rayalaseema, prioritising
Ananthapuramu, Sri Sathya Sai and YSR Kadapa districts, where groundwater tables
have fallen below 200 feet in 41 mandals.

### Minimum Support Price

A state Price Stabilisation Fund of Rs. 3,000 crore will guarantee procurement of
paddy, maize, groundnut, cotton and red gram at the announced minimum support
price. Procurement centres will operate within 5 kilometres of every gram
panchayat during the harvest window.

## Chapter 2 — Education

### Amma Vodi

Amma Vodi will continue at Rs. 15,000 per year per mother, and we will remove the
current one-child restriction. From the 2025-26 academic year the benefit applies
to every child enrolled from Class 1 to Class 12, capped at three children per
household. Eligibility requires 75 percent attendance and a household income below
Rs. 2.5 lakh per annum.

### School Infrastructure

Every government school will have functioning toilets, drinking water, a boundary
wall and a digital classroom by June 2026. In Visakhapatnam, NTR and Guntur
districts, 340 schools will be upgraded to English-medium CBSE-affiliated model
schools.

### Higher Education

Full fee reimbursement for students from families earning below Rs. 2.5 lakh per
annum, paid directly to the institution within 60 days of admission. Three new
government engineering colleges will be established, in Parvathipuram Manyam,
Alluri Sitharama Raju and Nandyal districts, which currently have the lowest
per-capita technical education capacity in the state.

## Chapter 3 — Healthcare

### Aarogyasri Expansion

The Dr. YSR Aarogyasri coverage limit rises from Rs. 5 lakh to Rs. 10 lakh per
family per year. The procedure list expands from 3,257 to 4,100 procedures,
adding paediatric cardiac surgery, cochlear implants and cancer immunotherapy.

### Primary Health

Each of the 1,145 Primary Health Centres will be staffed with two doctors, a
pharmacist and a lab technician, operating 24 hours in all mandal headquarters.
108 ambulance response time will be brought under 15 minutes in urban areas and
under 25 minutes in rural mandals.

A 300-bed super-speciality hospital will be built in Vijayawada, serving NTR,
Krishna, Eluru and Bapatla districts, at a cost of Rs. 680 crore.

## Chapter 4 — Employment and Industry

We commit to 6 lakh new jobs over five years:

- 2.1 lakh in manufacturing, anchored by the Visakhapatnam–Chennai Industrial
  Corridor and the Kopparthy node in YSR Kadapa district.
- 1.4 lakh in IT and IT-enabled services, with new technology parks in
  Visakhapatnam, Tirupati and Vijayawada.
- 1.1 lakh in food processing, with 24 mega food parks located in the districts of
  highest agricultural output.
- 1.4 lakh in construction, tourism and logistics.

The Andhra Pradesh Skill Development Corporation will train 5 lakh youth per year
with a guaranteed placement interview, and a stipend of Rs. 3,000 per month during
training.

## Chapter 5 — Women's Welfare

Self Help Group loans up to Rs. 5 lakh at zero interest, with the interest
subvention paid directly to the bank. The Disha Act enforcement machinery will be
strengthened with 26 district-level fast-track courts, one per district, and a
Disha police station in every revenue division.

Free RTC bus travel for all women within their district of residence, funded at
Rs. 2,150 crore per year.

## Chapter 6 — Housing and Infrastructure

### Housing

Twenty-five lakh pucca houses over five years, with a unit assistance of
Rs. 2.6 lakh in plain areas and Rs. 3.1 lakh in tribal and hilly mandals of Alluri
Sitharama Raju and Parvathipuram Manyam districts. Registration will be in the
name of the woman of the household.

### Roads

All 13,371 gram panchayats connected by an all-weather BT road by March 2028.
The Vijayawada eastern bypass and the Visakhapatnam outer ring road will be
completed as centrally sponsored projects with state land acquisition support.

### Power

Two hundred free units of electricity per month for households consuming under
300 units, and nine hours of assured free daytime power for agricultural
connections. Rooftop solar subsidy of 40 percent for the first 3 kilowatts.

## Chapter 7 — Pensions and Social Security

The monthly social security pension rises to Rs. 4,000 for the elderly, widows,
single women, weavers, toddy tappers and fishermen, and to Rs. 6,000 for persons
with disability above 80 percent and for patients with chronic renal failure,
thalassemia or paralysis.

Pension delivery continues at the doorstep on the first of every month by
Village and Ward Volunteers, before 11 a.m.

## Chapter 8 — Fisheries

Diesel subsidy of Rs. 12 per litre for mechanised boats, up to 3,000 litres per
boat per year. Four new fishing harbours at Machilipatnam, Narsapur, Juvvaladinne
and Uppada, and a fishermen's welfare fund of Rs. 500 crore for the annual fishing
ban period, paying Rs. 20,000 per family.

## Accountability

Progress against every commitment in this manifesto will be published quarterly on
a public dashboard, disaggregated by district and by assembly constituency.
