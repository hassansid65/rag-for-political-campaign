# Frequently Asked Questions — Voters

*Campaign FAQ · Andhra Pradesh People's Alliance*

Q1. How do I check whether my name is on the voter list?
A1. Visit the National Voters' Service Portal and search by your EPIC number or by
name and district, or send an SMS with your EPIC number to 1950. You can also ask
your Village or Ward Volunteer, who holds the part-wise electoral roll for your
polling area. Corrections must be filed on Form 8 before the roll closes.

Q2. I turned eighteen this year. How do I register to vote?
A2. File Form 6 online at the NVSP portal or in person at the office of the
Electoral Registration Officer for your assembly constituency. You need proof of
age, proof of ordinary residence, and a passport-size photograph. Registration
windows open on 1 January, 1 April, 1 July and 1 October each year.

Q3. What documents do I need to carry to the polling station?
A3. Your Elector Photo Identity Card is preferred. If you do not have it, any one
of these is accepted: Aadhaar, passport, driving licence, PAN card, MGNREGA job
card, bank or post office passbook with photograph, health insurance smart card
issued under the Ministry of Labour scheme, pension document with photograph, or a
service identity card issued by a government body.

Q4. I have moved to another district. Where do I vote?
A4. You vote where you are registered as an ordinary resident. If you have moved
permanently, file Form 8 for transposition to your new address. If your move is
recent and the roll has closed, you must vote at your old polling station for this
election.

Q5. How do I get a pension if I am eligible but not receiving one?
A5. Apply through your Village or Ward Volunteer, who will file it on the Grama or
Ward Sachivalayam portal. The Welfare and Education Assistant conducts a
verification visit. Approval is granted at the mandal level and the first payment
begins on the first day of the following month. If your application has been
pending for more than 45 days, call 1902 with your application number.

Q6. My Amma Vodi payment did not come this year. What do I do?
A6. The three most common reasons are attendance below 75 percent, a mismatch
between the child's Aadhaar and the school record, or the mother's bank account
being inactive. Ask the head teacher to show you the school's Amma Vodi social
audit list displayed each November, get the entry corrected, and escalate to the
Mandal Education Officer if it is not resolved within fifteen days.

Q7. Who is eligible for the free electricity benefit?
A7. Households with a domestic connection in their own name, consuming under 300
units per month, and holding a rice ration card. The first two hundred units each
month are free. If you cross 300 units in a month, that month is billed at the
normal tariff.

Q8. What is the difference between Rythu Bharosa and the crop insurance scheme?
A8. Rythu Bharosa is an unconditional annual investment support payment of
Rs. 18,000 per farmer family, paid whether or not the crop succeeds. Crop insurance
compensates you for a specific verified loss after a notified calamity, and the
premium is paid by the state on your behalf. You can receive both.

Q9. I am a tenant farmer without land in my name. Can I claim Rythu Bharosa?
A9. Yes, provided you hold a Crop Cultivator Rights Card for the current season.
The card is issued by the Village Revenue Officer with the landowner's
acknowledgement and is valid for eleven months. Take your Aadhaar and the CCRC to
the Rythu Bharosa Kendram in your panchayat.

Q10. How do I complain about a road, a water connection, or a street light?
A10. Register the grievance at your Grama or Ward Sachivalayam, or call 1902. You
will receive an acknowledgement number and a service-level deadline — 72 hours for
water and street lighting, 21 days for road repair estimates. Escalation is
automatic if the deadline is missed.

Q11. Is Aarogyasri accepted at private hospitals?
A11. Yes, at empanelled private hospitals. The list is displayed at every Primary
Health Centre and available on 104. Go to the Aarogyamitra desk inside the hospital,
not the billing counter. If a hospital refuses cashless treatment to an eligible
card holder, call 104 immediately and record the name of the person who refused.

Q12. What is the household income limit for welfare schemes?
A12. It varies by scheme. Aarogyasri uses Rs. 5 lakh per annum. Amma Vodi and fee
reimbursement use Rs. 2.5 lakh per annum. Pensions and housing use a monthly
threshold of Rs. 10,000 rural and Rs. 12,000 urban. Land holding limits of 3 acres
wet or 10 acres dry apply to pensions, Amma Vodi and housing.

Q13. Can I get a house site if I am landless?
A13. Yes. Landless eligible families receive a free house site of 1.5 cents along
with the housing unit assistance. The site and the house are registered in the name
of the woman of the household. Applications are made at the Sachivalayam and
verified against the landless survey.

Q14. How do I volunteer for the campaign?
A14. Visit your district campaign office or register at any Sachivalayam-adjacent
campaign desk. Volunteers are assigned by polling booth. There is no membership fee
and no obligation to campaign outside your own ward or panchayat.

Q15. When will results of my grievance be published?
A15. Grievance disposal statistics are published monthly on the public dashboard,
disaggregated by district, mandal and grievance category, along with the average
disposal time.

Q16. Does the campaign assistant on this phone line record what I say?
A16. The assistant transcribes your question in order to search the campaign's
published documents and answer you. It only answers from those documents. It does
not give legal advice, does not collect your vote preference, and cannot register
you as a voter — for that you must use Form 6 with the Election Commission.
